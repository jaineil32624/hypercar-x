Godot 4.3 app · Android & Windows

Drive it on your phone

Uses the Blender-baked model with VehicleBody3D physics fed by the engineering model. In-app it does 0-60 in 2.5 s, the ¼ mile in 8.35 s and tops out at 343 km/h, against the model's 2.10 s, 8.07 s and 343 km/h. Touch pads, a garage with pros and cons for every part, a ¼-mile gate, slalom, handling ring and jump ramp.

Swappable parts: pros & cons

Every option is a trade-off. Try them live in the simulator or the app.

Wheels
21" carbon −18 kg unsprung, sharper response · $22k, cracks on kerbs
18" rally soaks up rough roads · +22 kg, less grip
Tyres
Semi-slick +15% grip · unsafe in rain, 8k km life
Eco low-RR +8–10% range · longer braking
Aero
Track pack 2.5× downforce · more drag, less range
No wing lowest drag · rear goes light at speed
Motors
Quad 1450 kW sub-2 s 0-60 potential · traction limited, thermal derate
Dual 600 kW −48 kg, cheaper · no per-wheel torque vectoring
Battery
80 kWh LFP −90 kg, long cycle life · −25% range
130 kWh solid-state lighter and longer range · not production-ready
Brakes
Carbon-ceramic fade-free · $15k to replace
Cast iron cheap, strong cold bite · +20 kg unsprung, fades
